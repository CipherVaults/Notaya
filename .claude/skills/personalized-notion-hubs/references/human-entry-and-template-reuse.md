# Human Entry, Template Reuse and Semantic Preservation

Edition 3 method alignment. Read this when designing first-use/return/AI journeys, exposing reusable templates or restructuring a mature hub.

## Different readers, short useful routes

A newcomer needs purpose → one concrete example → next action. Show what they supply, what happens and the useful output. Label synthetic examples; do not turn examples into live progress. Explain essential terms before acronyms or template names.

A returning user needs the actual dated checkpoint and direct current-work route. A collaborating AI needs the operating contract, canonical sources, boundaries, required inputs and continuation. These are roles, not a requirement for three new pages or mandatory labels. Combine routes that do the same job.

Keep purpose and usable entry routes ahead of delivery notices, manifests and audit receipts. Put production material behind a clearly labeled maintenance route while preserving full direct access. Avoid container-only page chains. Do not hide prerequisites or the only active route inside history or several toggles.

## Long-page contents without losing the work route

When a long Human Guide, research page or Plan has an overwhelming TOC, put the native table of contents in a clearly labeled native toggle near the start, initially collapsed. Keep purpose → example → next action and the compact working/resume/AI routes visible. Short navigation need not be hidden, and the requested operative software board stays visible.

Preserve every heading, the full guide body and direct entrances. Test expanding/collapsing and representative exact heading links in the real rendering when available. A native TOC tag or text readback alone cannot establish that interaction or heading navigation. Apply this by page purpose, not as a universal layout.

## A template catalogue that actually works


Inventory every reusable artifact needed for the promised workflow, including cross-cutting Session, handoff, review and log templates where relevant. Reconcile unique identities and destinations, not counts alone. A duplicate can conceal an omission; an archive does not repair an incomplete catalogue.

Give each required template one canonical home, a short purpose/trigger, inputs, relationship to the next step and a direct exact open/copy/download route. Link it from both its workflow step and catalogue. Keep blanks, worked examples, completed records, prompts and historical editions distinguishable. A bundle is not an isolated template.

A label naming a particular blank must reach that blank or make it immediately selectable. Do not silently land users in a general room that requires searching. Preserve complete fields, Unicode, nested fences, anchors and adopted heading profiles; simpler teaching headings must not flatten literal templates.

## Findability and isolated reuse are different checks

Test finding the right artifact from Home without knowing its name, then obtaining and using the exact advertised artifact on its own. Check required companion material, references and version/source identity.

Record native Copy-control use, DOM/text extraction, individual download, and duplication/remapping separately. A working download or text extraction does not prove the Copy control worked. Compare complete bodies and state precise permitted normalization, such as a final newline. No universal click-count, time limit or page quota is implied.

For copied databases/templates, verify the new source/view/child identities and intended remapping before touching records. Preserve intentionally external evidence links. Do not claim duplication tests or automatic remapping that did not happen.

## Preserve meaning and ownership

Before restructuring, map source section/artifact → meaningful facts/rules/examples/conditions/responsibilities → exact destination → canonical owner → treatment and verification. Preserve useful source detail and full research through direct navigation and progressive disclosure before considering shortening.

A matching hash proves retention, not that a caveat or authority boundary still reaches the person who needs it. Brief linked orientation and dated snapshots may repeat facts purposefully; avoid competing owners for the same changing answer.

Check every created/changed route against its unique saved target and complete intended artifact. Use representative visual sampling for unchanged patterns, rather than treating it as changed-link coverage. Do not invent section fragments.

## Report separate evidence dimensions

Distinguish content/semantic preservation, saved native structure, human findability, isolated reuse, rendered desktop/narrow usability and owner acceptance. An agent walkthrough is not an uncoached human trial. Source text for a diagram is not rendered-diagram proof. If visual access is unavailable, state the exact remaining limit without pretending that connector configuration passes it.
