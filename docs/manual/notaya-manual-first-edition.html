Getting started

Notaya is a notes app for Windows. Your notes are saved on your own PC. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Two ways to install

  • Setup (Notaya-V1.5.1-setup.exe) installs Notaya for your Windows user. It does not ask for an administrator. It adds a Start menu entry and, if you tick it, a desktop shortcut. Your notes are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Notaya Notes. Uninstalling never deletes your notes.
  • Portable (Notaya-V1.5.1-portable.zip) needs no install. Unzip it anywhere and run Notaya\Notaya.exe. Your notes stay in a data folder next to the program, so you can carry them on a drive.

You need Windows 10 or 11 with the WebView2 runtime. If it is missing, Notaya tells you in a plain message box when it starts.

Good to know: the program is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Only one copy of Notaya runs at a time. Starting it again brings the running window to the front.

Your first start

  1. The first screen offers Start new notes or Import (to bring in notes from another app, or from a portable copy of Notaya).
  2. New notes open without a password. This is on purpose, so you can start writing at once. Your notes are stored in the encrypted format, but the key is built in, so they are not private yet. Turn on a password any time in Settings, under Security. See Privacy and backups.
  3. Notaya opens on Home. Make a notebook, add a section, and add a page.

How your notes are organized

NotebookThe biggest container, with a name, an emoji and a colour.
SectionA group of pages inside a notebook.
PageA document. A page can hold sub-pages, as deep as you like.
DatabaseA table of rows that can also be a board, calendar, chart and more.

Find your way around

A top bar, a sidebar on the left, and the page in the middle.

Top bar

The logo and name, a button to show or hide the sidebar, Back and Forward arrows, the search box (Ctrl+K), a Lock button (only when you have a password) and the Windows minimize, maximize and close buttons.

Sidebar

Home, Sticky notes and Settings at the top. Then your Favorites and your Notebooks, shown as a tree of notebooks, sections, pages and sub-pages. Databases appear in the tree next to pages. At the bottom you find New page, New database and Trash with a count. Drag the edge to resize it (200 to 420 pixels). Hide it with Ctrl+\.

Every row has a … menu. It appears when you point at the row. For a notebook it offers Add section, Edit notebook, Move up and down, Export and Delete. For a section: New page, New database, Rename, Move, Export and Delete. For a page: Add a page inside, Add a database inside, Add or remove favorite, Move up and down, Move to another section, Export and Move to trash.

  • Drag sections to reorder them inside a notebook. Drag pages to reorder them inside a section.
  • With the keyboard: arrow keys move through the tree, Enter opens, and Shift+F10 (or the Menu key) opens a row's menu.
  • Deleting a notebook asks first and makes a safety backup before it deletes anything.
Good to know: the right mouse button does not open these menus yet. Use the … button. Pages move between sections only through the "Move to" list, which shows the first 8 sections.

Home

A colourful banner that changes once a day, a greeting for the time of day, today's date, your Favorites (8), your Recent pages (8) and your Notebooks as cards (24). If you have no password, a one-time note reminds you that your notes are not private.

Search: Ctrl+K

One box searches pages, databases, database rows, notebooks and sticky notes as you type, and shows a short snippet for each hit. It matches every word you type, in any case. The same box lists actions: New page, New sticky note, Go to Notes, Appearance & settings, Export, Import pages, Back up vault now, and Lock vault.

Your last 5 searches appear under the box. Click the x to forget one, or choose "Clear recent searches". They are stored with your notes, encrypted when you have a password.

Good to know: opening a result takes you to the page but does not highlight the match inside it. There is no find bar for the open page yet (see Coming soon).

Back and forward

Go back with Backspace, Alt+←, the mouse's back button, or the arrow in the top bar. Go forward with Shift+Backspace, Alt+→, the forward button, or the arrow. Backspace does nothing while you are typing in a text box. Notaya remembers the last 100 places you visited.

Good to know: Notaya always opens on Home. It does not yet reopen the page you were on, and the back list is lost when you restart or lock.

Trash

Deleted pages and databases wait in Trash. You can Restore one, Delete forever, or Empty trash. Moving a page to Trash shows an Undo message first.

Notes and pages

Pages are made of blocks. Type to write, or press / to add something else.

The page header

Above the text you see the breadcrumb (notebook and section), the save state ("Saved & encrypted", or "Saved on this drive" without a password), a word count, the Favorite star, and a … menu with Move to section, Export, and Move to trash. Notaya saves as you type. There is no Save button.

Click Add icon on the title to pick one of about 600 emoji or upload a small picture. Click Add cover to pick a gradient, a colour or your own picture, and drag to reposition it.

Ways to add things

  • The "/" menu. Type / on an empty line. It groups about 26 items: Text, Lists, Insert, Database, Direction and Quick.
  • The ribbon. A strip above the page with tabs Home and Insert. A Table tab appears when the cursor is in a table. Collapse it and Notaya remembers.
  • The text toolbar. Select some text and a small toolbar appears: Turn into, font, size, bold, italic, underline, strike, code, 18 text colours, 18 highlight colours, link, alignment, equation, remove formatting.
  • Markdown typing. # then space makes a heading, - a bullet list, 1. a numbered list, > a quote, [ ] a check box, --- a divider, and three backticks a code block.
  • Drag handle. Point at a block, then drag its handle to move it, or open its menu.

Pictures

You can paste a picture, drop one on the page, or add one from the "/" menu or the ribbon. Then place it: drag it anywhere on the page and send it to the front or back, drag it left or right so text wraps around it, set its width as a percentage, crop it to a ratio (1:1, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16), align it, and add a caption. Notaya accepts PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP up to 25 MB each. SVG pictures are refused for safety.

Good to know: free placement is off inside columns, lists and tables. Exports place free pictures in the text flow.

Arabic and right-to-left writing

Each block picks its direction from the first strong letter, so Arabic and Hebrew blocks run right to left on their own. Shift+Enter starts a new line that can have its own direction. The ribbon and the "/" menu (Direction) can force a direction. Right-to-left works in pages and databases. A line's own direction is not kept in exports.

Favorites

Click the star in the page header, or use "Add to favorites" in the sidebar menu. Favorites show at the top of the sidebar and on Home.

Page templates

Row pages inside a database can be saved as a template. Templates for ordinary pages do not exist yet.

Good to know: older pages and imported pages can contain side-by-side columns and comment marks. They show and keep their content, but you cannot create new ones yet. There is no duplicate page, table of contents block, page history or backlinks yet.

Databases

A database is a table of rows. Each row is also a page. You can look at the same rows as a table, a board, a calendar, a timeline, a list, a gallery or a chart.

Make one

Use New database at the bottom of the sidebar, the "…" menu on a section or page, or "/" inside a page. Start blank, or from a CSV file. A database can be a full page or sit inside another page.

Good to know: once a vault holds a database, versions 1.4.0 and older cannot open it. Notaya makes a backup first.

There are 23 kinds. Pick one when you add a column.

Views

TableFreeze columns, wrap text, row numbers, resize and reorder, footers with calculations.
BoardCards in columns. Drag a card to change its group. Swimlanes.
CalendarMonth or week.
TimelineBars along dates, zoom, dependency arrows.
ListA compact list of titles.
GalleryCards with a cover.
ChartBar, line, donut or a single number.

A database can hold several views. Each view tab can be renamed, duplicated, moved or deleted. Every view has its own filters (nested and/or, including relative dates such as "this week"), sorts (several at once) and groups (including date and number buckets, and sub-groups).

Formulas, relations and rollups

Formulas use a language modeled on Notion's. Relations connect two databases both ways. Rollups summarize the rows you linked. The formula preview can lag by about a second while you type.

Rows as pages

Open a row as a full page, a side peek or a centre peek. Its properties sit above its text. Turn on sub-items to nest rows, and dependencies to link rows that must happen in order. Row templates give new rows a starting shape. Use "Save as template" on any row page.

Charts

The Chart view draws a bar, line or donut chart, or one big number. Choose what goes along the bottom (a property, with dates grouped by day, week or month), an optional second grouping (stacked or side by side), and what to measure: count, sum, average, median, minimum, maximum and more. Add a title, colour and labels. Click a bar, slice or point to see the rows behind it.

Good to know: each view shows one chart. There are no multi-chart dashboards or pie charts yet.

Automations and buttons

Automations run inside Notaya on a trigger: a row is added, a property changes, or a date arrives (with a number of minutes before or after). If the row matches a filter, Notaya can set a property, add a row, show a message or move the row to Trash. A Button property runs the same actions when you press it.

Good to know: automations run only while Notaya is open and unlocked. Date triggers catch up for up to 7 days afterwards. A chain can have 3 steps, and one run can add up to 50 rows. Reminders appear as a message inside Notaya. They are not Windows notifications.

Undo, lock and export

Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo up to 50 changes (cells, titles, new and deleted rows, row order). Undo is dropped when you close the last view of that database. The database menu also has Lock database (to stop edits), Import CSV, and export of the current view as CSV, Markdown, HTML, Word or PDF.

Sticky notes and calculator

Quick notes that float on your desktop, and a calculator you can open as many times as you like.

The sticky board

Open Sticky notes in the sidebar (Ctrl+3). Your notes appear as slightly tilted cards, newest edit first. Buttons: Select, Calculator, Open all, Close all and New note.

A sticky note on the desktop

Each note opens in its own small window that you can move and resize anywhere on the desktop. Notaya remembers where you put it. Choose a colour (Lemon, Blossom, Mint, Sky, Lavender, Peach, Cloud or Charcoal), make text bold or italic, add a check list, open a calculator, and switch on Keep on top so the note stays above other windows. Delete asks you to click twice. Closing a note keeps it.

Good to know: notes hide when your vault locks and come back when you unlock.

Select and delete several

Press Select (or Ctrl+click a card), tick cards by clicking or with Space, then use Select all, Select none or Delete. Notaya asks before it deletes, and does it in one step. Esc leaves select mode.

Calculators

Basic and scientific (sine, arcsine and so on, with Deg and Rad). Each calculator is its own small window that stays on top. Open as many as you want, from the board or from inside a sticky note. Calculations are worked out by Notaya's own code. They are not run as program text. Your history is shared between calculators (up to 500 lines) and saved with your notes, encrypted when you have a password. Copy copies only the result.

Good to know: the history can contain sums you would rather keep private. Clear it from the calculator.

Import and export

Bring notes in from other apps, and take them out in common formats.

Import pages

Open the import window from the hover icon in the Notebooks header, from "Import pages from another app" in an empty sidebar, or from Ctrl+K. Pick files or a whole folder, in any mix. Notaya reads Markdown, text, HTML, Word (.docx), PDF (text only), ZIP files (including exports from Notion and Notaya's own export) and folders. A progress bar and a Cancel button appear while it works. At the end you get a list of what was imported and anything left out, with the reason.

Imports always create a new notebook. Files up to 2 GB are accepted (4 GB unpacked from a ZIP).

Notion exports become real databases

If you import a Notion export (Markdown and CSV, HTML, or both together), Notaya turns tables into databases with Status, Number formats, Select, Multi-select and two-way relations. Row pages are folded into the rows. Icons, covers and call-outs come across.

Good to know: Notion's views and people do not carry over, and formulas arrive as their values. Video, audio and embeds are left out. Page order follows file names, not Notion's order. Importing the same export twice makes a second notebook. The import window still says "Databases (tables) are not imported". That sentence is out of date. They are imported.

Other imports

  • Import CSV in a database's menu adds rows (up to 2 GB, 100,000 rows and 100 columns).
  • Restore from backup and import from a portable copy are separate features. See Privacy and backups.

Export

Open Export from a page's … menu, a sidebar … menu (page, section or notebook), or Ctrl+K. Choose what to export: one page, chosen pages, a section, several sections, a notebook or several notebooks. Include sub-pages is on by default. Choose HTML, Markdown, PDF or Word, and one file or separate files in a ZIP (a ZIP can be imported again). Notaya opens the Save window itself.

PDF supports Arabic, Hebrew and Chinese, Japanese and Korean text. Web links in a PDF are clickable.

Good to know: exported files are not encrypted. Notaya warns you. A database placed inside a page is missing from page exports (a known issue, listed below). PDFs have no printed contents page and no clickable links between pages. Greek, Cyrillic and emoji can print as "?" in a PDF. Diagrams export as their code, not as a picture. Database views export from the database's own menu (CSV, Markdown, HTML, Word, PDF). There is no Excel file export yet.

Look and Windows

Open Settings from the sidebar or with Ctrl+,. It has sections for Appearance, Windows, Error log, Security, Backups and About.

Themes

Sunrise (the default, warm light with coral and amber), Sunrise dark, Black, Graphite, White, Paper, and Match Windows, which follows your system's light or dark setting.

Appearance

Accent colour (presets or your own), font (Inter, Segoe, Serif or Mono), text size (12 to 20), page width (Narrow, Normal, Wide or Full), density, window material (Solid, Mica or Acrylic), the lock-screen background and the colour new sticky notes start with.

Windows

  • Tray icon. Click it to open Notaya. Right-click for Open Notaya, Close sticky notes and Quit Notaya.
  • Close to tray. The close button hides Notaya instead of quitting. With a password, it locks first.
  • Start with Windows and Start minimized are switches in Settings.

Error log

Settings, Error log shows crash and error records kept on your PC. They never contain note text. You can open the folder.

About

Shows the version, where your notes live, how they are encrypted, and the licences of the open-source software Notaya is built on.

Privacy and backups

Security is optional. You decide whether your notes need a password.

Turn on a password

Settings, Security, Turn on. Choose a master password of at least 10 characters that Notaya rates "Strong". You can add a key file as well. Notaya re-encrypts your notes with the new key. Turn off needs your password. With security off, your notes are stored in the encrypted format with a public built-in key, so they are not confidential.

Good to know: if you forget the master password and have no key file, nobody can recover your notes, including us. Keep your backups.

How notes are protected

Each record is encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305. Your key comes from your password with Argon2id (a deliberately slow method that uses 256 MiB of memory), plus an optional key file. Any change to the file is detected. Keys stay inside Notaya's core and never reach the screen layer.

Notaya makes no connections to the internet for your notes. The page that displays Notaya allows only Notaya's own scripts and images. The Windows web component (WebView2) that draws the window may itself contact Microsoft when it starts.

Lock

Lock with the button in the top bar or Ctrl+L. Notaya also locks when you are idle (1 to 60 minutes, your choice), when you press Win+L, when Windows sleeps, when the drive is removed, and, if you choose, when you minimize. Every window saves first. Sticky notes hide while locked. Locking only exists when you have a password.

Screenshot protection

An optional switch (off by default, password only) that blocks screenshots of the Notaya window.

Backups

Notaya backs up automatically when you lock and when you quit (the newest 10 are kept), once an hour while the vault is open (3 kept), and before big deletions (5 kept). Settings, Backups also has Back up now, a list, Restore and a button to open the data folder.

Restore from backup and Start over are also on the lock screen. They never delete your current notes. Notaya sets them aside in a file named restore-undo- or set-aside-.

Good to know: when you turn on a password, Notaya offers to delete earlier unprotected backups, so none remain readable without it.

Limits and known issues

Things that do not work the way you might expect today. Several are scheduled to be fixed (see Coming soon).

AreaWhat to know
ExportsA database inside a page is silently left out of page exports. Export the database itself from its menu.
ImportAlways makes a new notebook. A second import of the same files duplicates them. The window's "databases are not imported" sentence is wrong.
MenusRight-click does not open Notaya menus. Use the … button.
SearchSearches every item in memory with no index, so very large vaults will feel slower. Results do not highlight the match in the page.
Find in pageNo Notaya find bar yet. Ctrl+F is not wired to Notaya's own search.
Start of the appAlways opens on Home, also after unlocking.
Notebook listThe "+ New notebook" and Import buttons in the Notebooks header show only when you point at it.
ChartsOne chart per view, no pie, no dashboards or slicers.
DatabasesThe timeline slows above about 500 rows. Calendar week has no hour grid. Files over a few hundred MB in a row are slow.
PicturesPNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP only. 25 MB each.
PDFNo contents page. Greek, Cyrillic and emoji can show as "?".
IconsSome icons are faint in dark themes. The Favorite star is hard to see when it is off.
Not tested on a real PCThe installers of versions 1.5.0 and 1.5.1, "Start with Windows" at a real sign-in, and the tray entry "Close sticky notes" have not been run in the real app. They are expected to work.

Coming soon

Nothing on this list is built yet. It is a draft plan, shown here so you know the direction. It is not a promise and has no dates. Items can move.

Where the plan lives: the full staging is in the version plan (draft pull request #62), file docs/plan/2026-11-07-version-plan.md. This manual gets a new edition for each release.

1.6 · The foundation planned

A safe way for an AI assistant such as Claude or Codex to read and edit your notes from outside, off by default, with deletes that always ask you. A log of who changed what, page version history, a recycle bin for everything, and the fixes in the table above (inline databases in exports, the import sentence, a visible New notebook button, Import and Export buttons in the top bar, clearer icons, reopening where you left off, dragging databases in the sidebar, names in About).

1.7 · Your app, your way planned

Right-click menus, a shortcuts page where you can change keys, a top bar you can customize, a richer tray menu, Ctrl+F as Notaya's own find bar with find and replace, your own Home banner picture per time of day, duplicate page.

1.8 · Pages that can hold your hubs planned

Columns you can make, coloured call-outs, folding headings, @ mentions with "linked from", links to a saved database view, a language picker and copy button on code blocks, diagrams as a code language with zoom and download, and a view of what changed between page versions.

1.9 · Tabs and files in and out planned

Page tabs, a PDF that reads like a book with a contents page, Excel (.xlsx) export for databases, databases inside notebook exports, import into an existing place, and re-import without duplicates.

1.10 · Corkboard, calculator, manual planned

A sticky-note corkboard with free layout, pins and strings, a calculator history panel and unit converters, this manual inside the app, a skippable tour, and a fuller About page.

1.11 to 1.12 · Dashboards planned

Dashboard pages with live tiles fed from your databases, slicers and click-through filters, and named reusable calculations.

1.13 to 1.14 · Whiteboard planned

A freeform canvas with pens (including pressure on a drawing tablet), shapes, templates, board sticky notes, and a timer.

1.15 · Notebook mode candidate for 2.0

A second layout you can choose per notebook, with coloured section tabs and pages that are free canvases.
