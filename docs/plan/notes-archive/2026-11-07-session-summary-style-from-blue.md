How these lessons combine with PBS v1.1: Notaya keeps its own adopted profile (one `docs/sessions.md`, newest on top, H1 session title, provenance block, the thirteen core sections at H2). The Blue lessons change how the sections are filled, not their names or order.

## What the Blue archive does well for discussion-heavy sessions

1. **The reasoning journey, not only the result.** Each change of direction is told as "X was proposed, Ahmed corrected Y, so Z", with the time. Example from FFmpeg: "Blue initially wanted an actual recurring failure before changing behavior; Ahmed rejected waiting ... The decision became explicit."
2. **Decisions carry status labels:** Confirmed, Provisional, Deferred, Rejected, and who decided. An assistant's proposal is never written as a decision.
3. **"Shifted from → to"** for every real change of framing, so a later reader does not revive an abandoned idea.
4. **The owner's own words are quoted** at the key moments, with times. Interpretation is marked as interpretation.
5. **Evidence verbs are exact:** proposed, saved, read back, tested, accepted are different claims. "Not established" is written when it is not.
6. **Current Position has Before / After / one-sentence Current standing,** written so a new session can resume from it alone.
7. **Open Questions are real questions,** with who can answer them and what they block; "no blocking question" is said when true.
8. **Confidence is split** by claim ("High for X; qualified for Y; unknown for Z"), with "Reason" and "Not higher because".
9. **Personal Note** only when there is real human meaning (for example Ahmed's tiredness, or his priority of practical usefulness), never invented.
10. **Deferred Work and Files sections** list what was intentionally left and where things live.
11. **Models, Effort, Roles and Provenance** for every actor, with "not independently established" where it is not known; the summary's own author is named separately from the work's actors.
12. **Time:** event timestamps with their source; wall-clock spans are not called active work.

## What Notaya adopts from this (first used in the 2026-11-07 entry, PR #56)

- A per-feature decision table: status (agreed / partly discussed / not discussed), what was agreed, Ahmed's reasons in his words, what exists today, and the coordinator's additions marked as such.
- A "Deferred or rejected along the way" list, so declined ideas (Favorites on the app bar, New notebook on the app bar, pins on the Windows desktop) are not proposed again.
- Numbered work blocks with time ranges in Actions Taken, each telling the back-and-forth.
- Plain words for Mohamed and for Ahmed (a non-developer); transcription errors quoted as written with their reading.

## What Notaya does not copy

- Blue's multi-project wrapper (H1 session, H2 project, H4 sections) and its archive indexes: Notaya is one project with its own adopted profile.
- Blue's very long evidence-boundary paragraphs: Notaya keeps a short "Record basis" paragraph.
- Blue's Cairo-time convention: Notaya records UTC.
