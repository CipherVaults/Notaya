## Calculator history of 500 (merged)
`CALC_HISTORY_MAX` in `src-tauri/src/model.rs` is 500 (was 200); the newest are kept, the oldest dropped. The history lives in the encrypted settings record: 500 entries of at most 2,000 characters is about 1 MB, far under the 32 MB item limit. The list already scrolled and "Clear" works. Earlier records that say 200 ([acceptance.md](../acceptance.md) X22, STATUS) describe what was true when written.

## Text colour and highlight (merged)
Text colour and highlight were one small pop-up; they are now two buttons in the ribbon and in the floating toolbar. Highlight offers nine matte colours (Stone, Clay, Peach, Butter, Sage, Mist, Lavender, Blush, Brick) and text colour nine mid-tone colours, each palette with a way to remove it ("Default text color", "No highlight"). Swatches have labels, the current choice is shown, and the arrow keys move between them. Highlights are saved as `data-color`. Contrast of the text colours against the six themes was computed: at least 3.2:1 on light and 4.1:1 on dark themes. Older saved colours still display. Sticky-note editors keep the plain highlight (not changed). Verified in the mock UI on five themes with screenshots and scripted clicks; **not run** in the real app.

The owner first asked that Notaya's own matte colours stay and that Notaya not look like a Notion copy (22:14:09, 22:16:22 UTC), then said Notion's colours may be added (22:17:28) and finally that they are merged into Notaya's own palette as normal colours without any Notion label (22:20:00). Built that way: `HIGHLIGHTS` and `TEXT_COLORS` in `src/lib/palette.ts` hold 18 colours in one fixed, mixed order (owner 22:21: shuffled, not grouped), nine of them with Notaya-style names (Ash, Cocoa, Amber, Honey, Moss, Denim, Plum, Orchid, Cherry) whose values are exactly Notion's gray, brown, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink and red, so an imported page keeps its colours exactly and they show as selected in the picker. The rest of the UI is Notaya's own.

## Export to Markdown, PDF and Word (merged, not accepted)
Asked 21:42:03 UTC. A page, chosen pages, a section or a whole notebook can be exported from the page, section and notebook menus, the sidebar and the command palette. One combined document (notebook, sections, pages, sub-pages one level deeper; trashed pages left out). Rust decrypts, opens the save dialog and writes the file; the UI only sends ids and options. A save location inside the data folder or a name ending in `.nota`/`.clf` is refused, an existing file is never replaced by the added extension, and the file is written to a temp file, synced and renamed. The dialog always warns that the exported file is not encrypted. Security row: P25 in `docs/security-design.md`.

- Markdown: GFM, pictures in a `<name>.assets/` folder.
- Word and PDF: hand-written ZIP/DEFLATE, OOXML and PDF 1.4 writers (no new library; `Cargo.toml` and `Cargo.lock` unchanged). Word supports right-to-left text. The PDF has bookmarks, links and page numbers.
- **PDF limit (owner chose to keep it, 22:25:27 UTC):** the PDF uses the standard fonts, so Arabic, Hebrew and Chinese/Japanese/Korean letters print as `?` and emoji are dropped; the app says how many characters were replaced. Word and Markdown keep every script. Other limits: GIF and WebP pictures are left out of PDFs (reported), equations export as TeX text, comments are not exported.
- Checked: 33 export tests and the whole suite (213) under Wine; sample PDF/DOCX/Markdown read by pdfinfo, pypdf, python-docx, LibreOffice and pandoc; mock UI dialog with Playwright. **Not run:** the real exe, the native save dialog, Microsoft Word, a Windows PDF viewer, `cargo clippy`, large pictures.
