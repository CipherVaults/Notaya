How a new reading gets in

  1. Cloud threads and the coordinator: copy the usage block of each session record into claude-sessions.csv.
  2. Sessions on the PC: run the scanner on the session's transcript and copy its line.
  3. Codex: after each run, write its tokens and the quota before and after.
  4. Limits: add a reading whenever someone looks at them, and once at each session stop.
  5. Rebuild this page from the CSV files and publish it to the same link.

⚠️ What is not measured

  • Mohamed's usage. His sessions run on his own account and are not visible from this side. Only Ahmed's account appears here.
  • Percentages of the limits. Session records say "allowed" and the reset time, not how full the window is. A reading from the Claude usage screen and Codex status is still needed for both weekly limits and the Codex 5-hour window.
  • Cost of PC sessions. Sessions on Gaming-PC have tokens but no dollar figure. Whether they draw from the cloud credit is not shown by any record.
  • The credit balance and exact expiry. Ahmed said "less than 24 hours" at 19:49 UTC on 2026-11-07. The 19:00 UTC time on 2026-11-08 is the coordinator's working assumption.
  • Before 2026-11-07 19:08 UTC. Never recorded and not reconstructed.
